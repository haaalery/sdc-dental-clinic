# SDC Dental Care: Refined Feature List

*Based on manager interview*

---

## Admin

### Financial Management

- Per-branch financial tracking and reporting
- Overall KPI dashboard with monthly and yearly inputs
- Total income view by day, week, month, and year
- Daily expense tracking
- Net income view (total income minus total expenses) for the day
- Income breakdown by payment method (Cash, Online, Card), including net totals per method
- Transparent calculations, with every computation viewable in the system

### Commission Management

- Configurable commission split between dentist and clinic (20%, 30%, 40%, or 50%)
- Fixed commission rate per treatment type
- Dentists record the treatments performed, which serves as the basis for commission computation
- View of each dentist's total earnings and commission share
- Filter by treatment and dentist

### Payments & Receipts

- Multiple payment options: Cash, Online, and Card
- Printable receipt for every patient transaction

---

## Front Desk

### Appointments & Scheduling

- Schedule and update patient appointments
- Patient-preferred dentist selection, with reassignment if the dentist is unavailable

### Payments & Finances

- Total payment per patient appointment
- Daily payment summary
- View of overall financial totals
- View of each dentist's total and commission share
- Remaining balance tracking per patient for ongoing treatments, with printable receipts
- Expense entry, including daily and miscellaneous expenses

### Patient Records

- Patient profile and treatment history (including attending dentist and dental concern)
- Occupation field added to patient registration
- File upload to patient profile (e.g., x-rays, documents)

### Inventory

- Supply usage tracking per patient, recorded by the assistant for each supply used

---

## Patient (Online Portal)

### Online Booking

- Online appointment booking
- Booking form fields: Name, Preferred Date and Time, Dental Concern, and Phone Number
